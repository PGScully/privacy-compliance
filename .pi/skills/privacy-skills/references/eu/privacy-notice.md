# EU — drafting a GDPR privacy notice

Use this when creating or updating a privacy notice (privacy policy) under the
GDPR. The notice is the main transparency instrument for **Art 12–14**; its content
depends on whether data are collected from the data subject (Art 13) or from
another source (Art 14).

## 1. Form and accessibility (Art 12)

- Concise, transparent, intelligible and easily accessible form, using **clear and
  plain language**, especially for children.
- In writing, or by other means including electronic; orally on request where
  identity is proven.
- **Free of charge**; respond to requests within **one month**, extendable by two
  further months for complex or numerous requests.
- May use standardised **icons** to give an overview of processing (Art 12(7)).

## 2. Content where data are collected from the data subject (Art 13)

**Art 13(1):**
- (a) identity and contact details of the controller and, where applicable, its
  representative;
- (b) contact details of the data protection officer (DPO), where applicable;
- (c) purposes of processing **and the legal basis** (Art 6);
- (d) where based on Art 6(1)(f), the **legitimate interests** pursued;
- (e) recipients or categories of recipients;
- (f) transfers to a third country/international organisation, and the adequacy
  decision or reference to Art 46/47 safeguards and how to obtain a copy.

**Art 13(2):**
- (a) retention period, or the criteria used to determine it;
- (b) the rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and
  portability;
- (c) where based on consent, the right to **withdraw consent at any time**;
- (d) the right to **lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority**;
- (e) whether provision is a statutory or contractual requirement (or necessary to
  enter a contract) and the consequences of not providing it;
- (f) the existence of **automated decision-making, including profiling** under
  Art 22, and meaningful information about the logic, significance and
  consequences.

**Art 13(3):** if the data will be further processed for a new purpose, provide
that information **before** the further processing.

**Art 13(4):** paragraphs 1–3 do not apply where and insofar as the data subject
already has the information.

## 3. Content where data were not obtained from the data subject (Art 14)

Adds to the Art 13 items:
- **categories of personal data** concerned (Art 14(1)(d));
- **the source** of the data and, if applicable, whether it came from publicly
  accessible sources (Art 14(2)(f));
- legitimate interests under Art 14(2)(b).

Timing (Art 14(3)): within a reasonable period and at the latest **one month**;
if used to communicate, at the latest at first communication; if disclosure to a
recipient is envisaged, at the latest at first disclosure. Exceptions in Art 14(5)
(already informed; impossible/disproportionate effort; required by law;
professional secrecy).

## 4. Cross-cutting content

- **Children (Art 8)** — for information society services offered to children,
  explain parental consent where the child is below the applicable age (16, or as
  low as 13 if Member State law provides).
- **Direct marketing (Art 21(2)–(4))** — state the **absolute right to object to
  direct marketing** (including related profiling), brought explicitly to the
  data subject's attention at first communication.
- **Joint controllers (Art 26)** — the essence of the arrangement must be made
  available.
- **Representative (Art 27)** — contact details where the controller is not
  established in the Union.
- **Automated decisions (Art 22)** — explain the logic, significance and envisaged
  consequences, and the right to human intervention, to express a view and to
  contest the decision.
- **AI Act (Art 50)** — where AI systems interact with people, generate synthetic
  content, or perform emotion recognition or biometric categorisation, include the
  relevant transparency disclosures.

## 5. Drafting checklist

- [ ] Controller identity, contact details and representative — **Art 13(1)(a)**.
- [ ] DPO contact — **Art 13(1)(b)**, Art 37.
- [ ] Purposes and legal basis for each processing activity — **Art 13(1)(c)**, Art 6.
- [ ] Legitimate interests where relied on — **Art 13(1)(d)**, Art 14(2)(b).
- [ ] Recipients or categories — **Art 13(1)(e)**.
- [ ] Transfers, adequacy/safeguards and how to obtain a copy — **Art 13(1)(f)**,
      Art 44–49.
- [ ] Retention period or criteria — **Art 13(2)(a)**.
- [ ] Rights: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability —
      **Art 13(2)(b)**, Art 15–20.
- [ ] Withdrawal of consent — **Art 13(2)(c)**, Art 7(3).
- [ ] Right to complain to a supervisory authority — **Art 13(2)(d)**.
- [ ] Whether provision is required and the consequences — **Art 13(2)(e)**.
- [ ] Automated decision-making/profiling disclosure — **Art 13(2)(f)**, Art 22.
- [ ] Source and categories of data where not collected from the data subject —
      **Art 14(1)(d)**, **Art 14(2)(f)**.
- [ ] Layered/linked notice for Art 14 timing — **Art 14(3)**.
- [ ] Children and parental consent where applicable — **Art 8**.
- [ ] Direct marketing objection — **Art 21(2)–(4)**.
