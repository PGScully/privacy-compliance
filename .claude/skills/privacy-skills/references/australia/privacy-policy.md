# Australia — drafting an APP privacy policy

Use this when creating or updating an APP privacy policy for an APP entity under
the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). The policy is required by **APP 1.3** and its contents
by **APP 1.4**. A collection notice (APP 5) is a separate, shorter artefact — see
below.

## 1. The obligation

- **APP 1.3:** the entity must have a **clearly expressed and up-to-date policy**
  about the management of personal information (the APP privacy policy).
- **APP 1.5:** take reasonable steps to make it available **free of charge** and in
  an appropriate form.
- **APP 1.6:** if asked for a copy in a particular form, take reasonable steps to
  provide it in that form.
- **APP 1.2:** the policy sits within practices, procedures and systems that ensure
  compliance and enable inquiries and complaints to be handled.

## 2. Required contents (APP 1.4)

The policy **must** contain all of the following:

| Ref | Required content |
|---|---|
| 1.4(a) | the kinds of personal information the entity collects and holds |
| 1.4(b) | how the entity collects and holds personal information |
| 1.4(c) | the purposes for which it collects, holds, uses and discloses personal information |
| 1.4(d) | how an individual may access their personal information and seek correction |
| 1.4(e) | how an individual may complain about a breach of the APPs (or a binding registered APP code), and how the entity will deal with complaints |
| 1.4(f) | whether the entity is likely to disclose personal information to overseas recipients |
| 1.4(g) | if so, the countries in which recipients are likely to be located, if practicable to specify |

## 3. Common additional content

Include where relevant (not strictly required by APP 1.4 but expected or driven by
other provisions):

- **Sensitive information** — call out health, biometric, genetic and other
  sensitive categories separately, since APP 3.3 imposes a higher bar.
- **Collection notices (APP 5.2)** — the matters the entity must notify at or before
  collection: identity and contact details; third-party collection; legal
  requirement/authorisation; purposes; main consequences of not providing; usual
  disclosure recipients; that the policy covers access/correction; that the policy
  covers complaints; overseas disclosure and countries (APP 5.2(a)–(j)).
- **Direct marketing (APP 7)** — how to opt out, that opt-out is free and prompt,
  and how to request the source of the individual's information (APP 7.6–7.7).
- **Overseas disclosure (APP 8)** — if relying on informed consent, the entity must
  expressly inform the individual that APP 8.1 will not apply and obtain consent
  (APP 8.2(b)). State the countries where practicable.
- **Government related identifiers (APP 9)** — if used, state the purpose.
- **Access and correction mechanics (APP 12, 13)** — how to make a request, the
  response timeframe (30 days for agencies; reasonable period for organisations),
  and that charges do not apply to making a request (and, for organisations, must
  not be excessive).
- **Complaints (APP 1.4(e))** — the internal process and the individual's right to
  complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
- **Retention and destruction (APP 11.2)** — how long information is kept and how it
  is destroyed or de-identified.
- **Credit reporting or APP codes** — if the entity is a credit reporting body or is
  bound by a registered APP code, add the code-specific disclosures.
- **Notifiable data breaches (Part IIIC)** — note how the entity would notify an
  eligible data breach, if described in the policy.

## 4. Drafting checklist

Validate the draft against every row before returning it:

- [ ] Identity and contact details of the entity (and any representative).
- [ ] Kinds of personal information collected and held, with sensitive categories
      called out — **APP 1.4(a)**.
- [ ] How it is collected and held (sources, systems, security summary) —
      **APP 1.4(b)**.
- [ ] Purposes of collection, holding, use and disclosure — **APP 1.4(c)**.
- [ ] Access and correction — **APP 1.4(d)**, APP 12, APP 13.
- [ ] Complaints process and OAIC reference — **APP 1.4(e)**.
- [ ] Overseas disclosure: yes/no and countries where practicable — **APP 1.4(f)**,
      **APP 1.4(g)**, APP 8.
- [ ] Collection notice matters covered (or a linked notice) — **APP 5.2**.
- [ ] Direct marketing opt-out and source request — **APP 7**.
- [ ] Retention/destruction — **APP 11.2**.
- [ ] Policy is dated, versioned and available free of charge — **APP 1.3**, APP 1.5.

## 5. Note

Where the entity is also subject to the EU GDPR, use
