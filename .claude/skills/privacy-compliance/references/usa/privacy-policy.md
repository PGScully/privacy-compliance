# USA / California — drafting CCPA disclosures

Use this when creating or updating CCPA disclosures. The CCPA requires several
distinct notices, each with prescribed content. Cite as **CCPA § 1798.xxx** and
**CCPA Regs § 70xx**. See `ccpa.md` for the obligations these notices support.

## 1. The notices

| Notice | Required by | When |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy policy | § 1798.130(a)(5); Regs § 7011 | Always (at least every 12 months) |
| Notice at Collection | § 1798.100(a); Regs § 7012 | At or before collection |
| Notice of Right to Opt-out of Sale/Sharing (+ "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link) | § 1798.135(a)(1); Regs § 7013 | If the business sells/shares |
| Notice of Right to Limit (+ "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information" link) | § 1798.135(a)(2); Regs § 7014 | If sensitive PI is used/disclosed beyond permitted purposes |
| Alternative Opt-out Link ("Your Privacy Choices") | Regs § 7015 | Optional, in lieu of the two opt-out links |
| Notice of Financial Incentive | § 1798.125(b)(2); Regs § 7016 | If offering a financial incentive or price/service difference |
| Pre-use Notice for ADMT | Regs § 7220 | Before using ADMT for a significant decision |

## 2. Privacy policy content (Regs § 7011(e))

**Information practices:**
- categories of personal information collected in the preceding 12 months, using the
  statutory category terms (§ 1798.140(v)(1)(A)–(K), (ae)(1)–(2)) — § 7011(e)(1)(A);
- categories of sources — § 7011(e)(1)(B);
- the specific business or commercial purposes for collection — § 7011(e)(1)(C);
- categories sold or shared in the preceding 12 months, or a statement that none
  were — § 7011(e)(1)(D);
- for each such category, the categories of third parties — § 7011(e)(1)(E);
- the specific purposes for selling/sharing — § 7011(e)(1)(F);
- whether the business has **actual knowledge** it sells/shares the PI of consumers
  under 16 — § 7011(e)(1)(G);
- categories disclosed to service providers/contractors for a business purpose, or a
  statement that none were — § 7011(e)(1)(H);
- the specific business/commercial purposes for that disclosure — § 7011(e)(1)(I);
- whether sensitive PI is used or disclosed beyond the permitted purposes —
  § 7011(e)(1)(J).

**Consumer rights** — § 7011(e)(2): know/access, delete, correct, opt out of
sale/sharing (if applicable), limit sensitive PI (if applicable), opt out of ADMT
(if applicable), access ADMT (if applicable), and non-retaliation.

**How to exercise rights** — § 7011(e)(3): methods; instructions and portal links;
the opt-out and limit notice contents or links; the verification process; how
opt-out preference signals are processed; authorized-agent instructions; processes
for under-16 consumers where known; and a contact.

**Other:** date last updated — § 7011(e)(4); large-volume metrics or a link
(§ 7102) — § 7011(e)(5).

## 3. Notice at Collection content (Regs § 7012(e))

- categories of personal information (including sensitive PI) to be collected;
- the purposes for which they are collected and used;
- whether each category is **sold or shared**;
- the **retention period** for each category, or the criteria used;
- a link to the Notice of Right to Opt-out of Sale/Sharing (if selling/sharing);
- a link to the privacy policy.

Placement: readily available where consumers encounter it at or before collection
(webpages, forms, apps, paper, signage, orally) — § 7012(c). A link may point
directly to the relevant section of the privacy policy, not the top — § 7012(f).
Do not collect if the notice is not given — § 7012(d).

## 4. Opt-out and limit notices

- **Notice of Right to Opt-out** — describe the right and give instructions and the
  interactive form — § 7013(f). Conspicuous link at header/footer — § 7013(c). No
  notice needed if the business does not sell/share **and** says so — § 7013(g).
- **Notice of Right to Limit** — describe the right and give instructions — § 7014(f).
  No notice needed if sensitive PI is used only for the permitted purposes (or
  without inferring characteristics) **and** the policy says so — § 7014(g).
- **Alternative Opt-out Link** — title "Your Privacy Choices" or "Your California
  Privacy Choices" with the opt-out icon — § 7015(b).

## 5. Financial incentive notice (Regs § 7016(d))

Summary of the incentive; material terms including categories of PI and the value
of the consumer's data; how to opt in; the right to withdraw and how; and an
explanation of how the price/service difference is reasonably related to the data's
value, with a good-faith estimate and calculation method.

## 6. Pre-use Notice for ADMT (Regs § 7220(c))

- plain-language, specific purpose;
- the right to opt out and how to submit a request (or, under the human-appeal or
  other exception, the appeal route or the exception relied on);
- the right to access ADMT and how to request it;
- that retaliation is prohibited;
- additional layered information on how the ADMT works, the type of output, how the
  decision is made (including any human role), and the alternative process if the
  consumer opts out.

A Pre-use Notice may be combined with the Notice at Collection — Regs § 7220(a).

## 7. Form, accessibility and maintenance

- Plain, straightforward language; readable on small screens; available in the
  languages the business ordinarily uses; accessible (e.g. WCAG 2.1) — Regs § 7003.
- Conspicuous "privacy" link on homepages or app download/landing pages and in the
  app's settings menu — Regs § 7011(d).
- Available in a printable format — Regs § 7011(c).
- Update the policy at least once every 12 months and show the date — §
  1798.130(a)(5); Regs § 7011(e)(4).

## 8. Drafting checklist

- [ ] Categories of PI and sensitive PI use the statutory category terms —
      Regs § 7011(e)(1)(A).
- [ ] Categories of sources and specific purposes — Regs § 7011(e)(1)(B)–(C).
- [ ] Sold/shared categories and third parties, or an explicit "none" statement —
      Regs § 7011(e)(1)(D)–(F).
- [ ] Under-16 sale/share actual-knowledge statement — Regs § 7011(e)(1)(G).
- [ ] Business-purpose disclosure categories — Regs § 7011(e)(1)(H)–(I).
- [ ] Sensitive PI statement — Regs § 7011(e)(1)(J).
- [ ] All applicable rights explained — Regs § 7011(e)(2).
- [ ] Methods, portal, verification, opt-out preference signals, authorized agents,
      contact — Regs § 7011(e)(3).
- [ ] Date last updated — Regs § 7011(e)(4).
- [ ] Large-volume metrics if applicable — Regs § 7011(e)(5), § 7102.
- [ ] Notice at Collection with retention and sell/share status — Regs § 7012(e).
- [ ] Opt-out / limit / alternative links and notices as applicable —
      Regs §§ 7013–7015.
- [ ] Financial incentive notice — Regs § 7016.
- [ ] ADMT Pre-use Notice — Regs § 7220.

## 9. Note

Where the business is also subject to the GDPR, merge the CCPA disclosures with the
GDPR Art 13/14 notice (`references/eu/privacy-notice.md`) — for example, a single
privacy policy with a clearly labelled California section plus the required links
and a separate Notice at Collection where the point of collection requires it.
