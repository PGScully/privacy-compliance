# USA / California — CCPA obligations

Obligation-by-obligation reference. Cite as **CCPA § 1798.xxx** (Civil Code) and
**CCPA Regs § 70xx** (Title 11, Div 6). See `references/usa/README.md` for scope and
definitions, `privacy-policy.md` for policy/notice content, and `audit-checklist.md`
for audit procedures.

## 1. Collection, use and retention limits

- **Notice at or before collection** of the categories of personal information and
  the purposes, whether it is sold or shared, the categories of sensitive personal
  information and their purposes, and the **retention period or criteria** —
  § 1798.100(a). Do not collect new categories or use data for incompatible
  purposes without a new notice — § 1798.100(a)(1)–(2); Regs § 7002(f).
- **Purpose limitation and proportionality** — collection, use, retention and
  sharing must be reasonably necessary and proportionate to a disclosed purpose
  consistent with consumer expectations, or a compatible purpose — § 1798.100(c);
  Regs § 7002. For any other purpose, obtain **consent** (Regs § 7002(e), § 7004).
- **No dark patterns** in obtaining consent or in request methods — § 1798.140(h);
  Regs § 7004.
- **Contracts** with third parties / service providers / contractors — § 1798.100(d)
  (see § 5 below).
- **Reasonable security** for personal information per Civil Code § 1798.81.5 —
  § 1798.100(e).

## 2. Consumer rights and handling

| Right | Statute | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Know / access | §§ 1798.110, 1798.115 | Respond **within 45 days**, extendable once by 45 days with notice — § 1798.130(a)(2)(A); Regs § 7024. Free of charge; disclosures must be portable — § 1798.130(a)(3)(B)(iii) |
| Delete | § 1798.105 | 45 days; propagate to service providers/contractors and notify third parties sold/shared to — § 1798.105(c); Regs § 7022 |
| Correct | § 1798.106 | 45 days; commercially reasonable efforts — Regs § 7023 |
| Opt out of sale/sharing | § 1798.120 | Comply **as soon as feasibly possible, no later than 15 business days** — Regs § 7026 |
| Limit use of sensitive PI | § 1798.121 | 15 business days — Regs § 7027 |
| Opt out of ADMT | Regs §§ 7200, 7221 | Pre-use notice and opt-out (or human appeal); 15 business days — § 7221(n) |
| Access ADMT | Regs § 7222 | Plain-language explanation of purpose, logic, outcome |
| No retaliation | § 1798.125 | See § 6 below |

- **Two or more designated request methods**, at least one reflecting how the
  business primarily interacts with consumers; toll-free number minimum for
  non-online-only businesses — § 1798.130(a)(1); Regs § 7020.
- **Opt-out preference signals** must be honoured and processed (including
  frictionlessly where the business opts into that approach) — § 1798.135(b);
  Regs § 7025.
- **Do not require account creation** or unnecessary information to submit an
  opt-out/limit request — § 1798.135(c); Regs §§ 7026, 7027.
- **Verification** must be reasonable and proportionate — Regs §§ 7060–7063;
  **authorized agents** — Regs § 7063. Verification data may only be used for
  verification — § 1798.130(a)(7).
- **12-month wait** before re-requesting consent after an opt-out/limit —
  §§ 1798.125(b)(3), 1798.135(c)(4).

## 3. Disclosures and notices

Required disclosures (Regs § 7010):

- **Privacy policy** — § 1798.130(a)(5); Regs § 7011.
- **Notice at Collection** — § 1798.100(a); Regs § 7012.
- **Notice of Right to Opt-out of Sale/Sharing** and the "Do Not Sell or Share My
  Personal Information" link — § 1798.135(a)(1); Regs § 7013.
- **Notice of Right to Limit** and the "Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal
  Information" link — § 1798.135(a)(2); Regs § 7014.
- **Alternative Opt-out Link** — Regs § 7015.
- **Notice of Financial Incentive** — § 1798.125(b)(2); Regs § 7016.
- **Pre-use Notice** for ADMT — Regs §§ 7010(c), 7220.

Content requirements are detailed in `references/usa/privacy-policy.md`.

## 4. Minors

- A business must not **sell or share** the personal information of consumers it
  has actual knowledge are **under 16** without affirmative authorisation: the
  consumer for 13–16, or a parent/guardian under 13; willful disregard is treated
  as actual knowledge — § 1798.120(c).
- Opt-in process and notices — Regs §§ 7070–7072.

## 5. Service providers, contractors and third parties

- **Contracts** with service providers/contractors must prohibit selling/sharing,
  specify limited and specific business purposes (not generic), prohibit use
  outside the direct business relationship, require the same level of privacy
  protection, grant audit/stop-and-remediate rights, require breach-of-capability
  notification, and enable consumer requests — Regs § 7051; § 1798.100(d).
- **Third-party agreements** must identify limited/specified purposes and impose
  equivalent obligations — Regs § 7053.
- **Due diligence** matters: failing to enforce contract terms can undermine the
  defence that the business had no reason to believe the recipient would misuse
  the data — Regs §§ 7051(c), 7053(b).
- Subcontractors must be bound by equivalent terms — Regs § 7051(b).

## 6. Non-discrimination and financial incentives

- No discrimination for exercising rights (denial, different price/quality,
  retaliation against employees/applicants/contractors) — § 1798.125(a);
  Regs § 7080.
- Financial incentives allowed if reasonably related to the value of the data,
  with **prior opt-in**, described material terms, revocable, no unjust/coercive
  practices, and a Notice of Financial Incentive — § 1798.125(b); Regs §§ 7016,
  7081.

## 7. Security and breach

- Reasonable security procedures appropriate to the information —
  § 1798.100(e); Civil Code § 1798.81.5.
- **Private right of action** for a data breach of non-encrypted and
  non-redacted personal information resulting from a failure to maintain
  reasonable security — § 1798.150 (statutory damages per consumer per incident).

## 8. Employee, applicant and B2B data

The former employee/B2B exemptions were removed. Employee, applicant and
independent-contractor personal information is generally **in scope** for rights
and notices (note the limited compensation/HR exception to risk assessments at
Regs § 7150(b)(2)(A)). Employer non-retaliation is express — § 1798.125(a)(1)(E).

## 9. Governance: training and record-keeping

- **Training** of everyone handling inquiries or compliance — § 1798.130(a)(6);
  Regs § 7100.
- **Record-keeping** of how requests are handled — Regs § 7101.
- **Large-volume businesses** (10,000,000+ consumers): compile and publish annual
  request metrics by **1 July** each year — Regs § 7102.

## 10. Cybersecurity audits (Regs Article 9, §§ 7120–7124)

- Required where processing presents **significant security risk** — thresholds at
  § 7120(b) (e.g. the § 1798.140(d)(1) revenue threshold plus 250,000+ consumers or
  50,000+ consumers' sensitive PI).
- **Phased first reports**: 1 April 2028 (2026 revenue > $100M), 2029 ($50M–$100M),
  2030 (< $50M); annual thereafter — Regs § 7121.
- Must be performed by a **qualified, objective, independent** auditor using
  accepted standards; management assertions alone cannot support findings;
  independence rules for internal auditors — Regs § 7122.
- Scope and report contents — Regs § 7123; certification — § 7124; retain audit
  documents **5 years** — § 7122(g).

## 11. Risk assessments (Regs Article 10, §§ 7150–7157)

- Required **before** processing that presents significant privacy risk:
  selling/sharing; processing sensitive PI; using ADMT for a significant decision;
  certain inferences about work/education performance or from sensitive locations;
  and processing PI to train ADMT or facial/emotion-recognition technology —
  Regs § 7150(b).
- Must document purpose (not generic), categories of PI, operational elements,
  risks and benefits, and safeguards; the assessment weighs risks against
  benefits — Regs §§ 7152, 7154.
- Stakeholder involvement — § 7151; timing/retention — § 7155; comparable-set and
  other-law options — § 7156; **submit to the CPPA on request** — § 7157.

## 12. Automated decisionmaking technology (Regs Article 11, §§ 7200, 7220–7222)

- Applies to ADMT used for a **significant decision** about a consumer —
  § 7200(a); compliance required from **1 January 2027** — § 7200(b).
- **Pre-use notice** (may be combined with the Notice at Collection) describing the
  specific purpose, the opt-out and access rights, the anti-retaliation rule, and
  how the ADMT works and how the decision would otherwise be made —
  § 7220(c), layered notice permitted.
- **Opt-out** with two or more methods (at least one matching the primary
  interaction); **human appeal** and two admission/hiring and allocation
  exceptions — § 7221(b); comply within **15 business days** — § 7221(n).
- **Access** to ADMT: purpose, logic, outcome, and how to appeal — § 7222.

## 13. Exemptions

- § 1798.145 covers, among others: protected **free speech** and journalism;
  **FCRA**-regulated activity; **HIPAA**-covered medical information; **CMIA**;
  **GLBA**; **Driver's Privacy Protection Act**; **deidentified/aggregate** data;
  and information used for **security/integrity** and certain internal uses.
- Note the public-interest/security exceptions are narrowly drafted; assess each.

## 14. Enforcement

- **CPPA administrative fines** up to **$2,500 per violation** ($7,500 for
  intentional violations or violations involving consumers known to be under 16),
  as adjusted — § 1798.155.
- **Private right of action** for data breaches — § 1798.150.
- The CPPA may investigate, issue stipulated orders and conduct audits —
  Regs §§ 7300–7304.

## 15. Engineering signals and common failures

| Signal | Check |
|---|---|
| Consent banner / GPC support | Honour opt-out preference signals (Regs § 7025); do not treat cookies as ADMT opt-out |
| Ad tech, pixels, "share" flows | Sale/share definition (§ 1798.140(ad), (ah)); opt-out within 15 business days |
| Sensitive PI (precise geolocation, health, credentials) | Limit right (§ 1798.121); risk assessment (§ 7150) |
| Retention config | State period or criteria in notice (§ 1798.100(a)(3)); Regs § 7002 |
| Vendor/processor contracts | Regs §§ 7051, 7053 mandatory terms; audit rights |
| Profiling / scoring / automated decisions | ADMT regime (Regs §§ 7200, 7220); risk assessment |
| Access/delete/correct endpoints | 45-day clock; propagate deletion to downstream recipients |
| Security programme | Cybersecurity audit readiness (Regs §§ 7120–7124) |

**Common failures:** no retention period in the notice; not honouring GPC/opt-out
preference signals; failing to propagate deletion downstream; generic contract
purposes; ignoring employee/applicant data; no ADMT or risk-assessment process; not
updating the privacy policy every 12 months (§ 1798.130(a)(5)).
